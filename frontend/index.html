About ResearchLens

ResearchLens is an evidence-grounded research assistant. Upload your source documents, ask a question, and get an answer built only from what those sources — and optionally live web results — actually say, with every claim tied back to a numbered citation.

🧠 Retrieval

Uploaded files are chunked and ranked against your question using TF-IDF and cosine similarity — fast, deterministic, and easy to reason about.

📎 Citations

Every claim in an answer is linked to a [Source N] so you can verify exactly where it came from.

🌐 Web Search

When your uploaded sources don't fully cover the question, ResearchLens can optionally pull in live DuckDuckGo results to fill the gaps.

⚠️ Honest Gaps

If something can't be answered from the available sources, ResearchLens says so explicitly instead of guessing.
